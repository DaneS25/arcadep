# Assets

Put downloaded assets here. Everything must be CC0 or SIL Open Font License; no account-locked or paid assets. The site works without any of them (plain fallbacks) and looks better once they are added.

## textures/

From [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com), 1K JPG. Keep the Color, NormalGL and Roughness maps for each:

- a concrete wall
- a brick wall (graffiti alley)
- wet asphalt or road
- rusty corrugated or painted metal
- a metal grate or floor plate

## fonts/

From [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com), `.woff2` or `.ttf`:

- Orbitron (neon signs)
- Share Tech Mono (terminal and CRT)
- Permanent Marker (graffiti)

## thumbnails/

A screenshot of each project for the graffiti wall, about 512 px wide. Dummy placeholders are generated until then.

## Optional, later

A night-sky or city backdrop image for the moving background.
